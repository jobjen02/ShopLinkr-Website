<p><strong>Multichannel inventory management is running one stock across several sales channels at once, so every sale updates your levels on all channels instantly. That prevents overselling, lets you process every order from one overview, and lets you scale without the manual work catching up with you.</strong></p>

<p>If you sell through bol, your own Shopify or WooCommerce webshop, and maybe Kaufland too, you list the same products on every channel but hold only one physical stock. Without a central system that means retyping levels by hand, and one missed update already leads to a sale you cannot fulfill. Multichannel inventory management solves this by connecting your channels to a single source.</p>

<h2>Why multichannel inventory management</h2>
<p>Selling across channels in isolation does not scale. The problems pile up as your volume grows:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Overselling</strong>: sell the same product on two channels at almost the same moment and you sell something that is gone. That costs cancellations, negative reviews, and on marketplaces your seller score.</li>
<li><strong>Fragmented orders</strong>: every order in a different screen, with its own exports and separate shipping tools.</li>
<li><strong>Lost time</strong>: updating levels, retyping orders, and creating labels one by one takes more time per channel every week.</li>
</ul>
