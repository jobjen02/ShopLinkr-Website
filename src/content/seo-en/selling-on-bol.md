<h2>Creating a seller account</h2>
<p>To sell as a business, you need a <a href="https://www.kvk.nl/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Chamber of Commerce registration</a>. After that, you work through these steps:</p>
<ol>
<li>Go to bol.com and register as a seller.</li>
<li>Enter your business and bank details and confirm your account.</li>
<li>Add your first products with a clear title, description, and good photos.</li>
<li>Set your stock, price, and delivery time, then publish the listings.</li>
</ol>
<p>Solid product information is your most important sales tool. A sharp title, complete specifications, and clear photos decide whether a buyer picks your offer.</p>
<p>Alongside your Chamber of Commerce registration, bol.com expects a valid VAT number and the ability to communicate with your customers in Dutch. When registering you also provide information about your catalog, such as categories and brands. Bol.com reviews your details, and once you are approved you can list your first products.</p>

<h2>Choosing the right products and listing them well</h2>
<p>Not every product sells equally well. Do your homework first: look at demand, study your competitors, and find niches where the margin is still healthy. Then run the numbers honestly and include purchasing, storage, packaging, and shipping. Only once those costs are covered do you know whether a product is profitable.</p>
<p>Once your product is online, your listing decides whether people click and buy. Put your main keywords in the title and description, choose the right category, and use sharp, honest photos, so buyers can find your product more easily.</p>
